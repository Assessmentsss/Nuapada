School-wise & Student-wise Report Cards

Every child assessed on 10 foundational literacy & numeracy competencies is placed into one of three performance bands, shown here as a symbol. Tap a block on the map or open a school in the list below to see its overall (school-wise) results, then switch to the Student-wise tab and click a roll number to see exactly which questions that child answered correctly and which they didn't.

How to read the symbols

Every competency result — for a single child or for a whole school — is shown using one of these three symbols instead of raw scores, so results are easy to scan at a glance.

Phala — AchievedThe child performed this competency correctly / at the expected level.
Phula — ProgressingThe child showed partial / emerging skill on this competency.
Kadha — Needs SupportThe child needs targeted support on this competency.
How school-wise figures are calculated: for each competency, every assessed child's result is classified into Phala / Phula / Kadha. The school-wise bar shows what share (%) and count of children in that school fall into each band for that competency.

How student-wise figures are calculated: each child's own result is classified into a single band (Phala / Phula / Kadha) per competency. Click a roll number in the Student-wise tab to see the actual questions asked and exactly which ones that child got right (✓) or wrong (✗).

Source data: PARAKH 2027 Baseline Tools: Grade 2 – Nuapada (cleaned assessment sheet, collected 21–28 Sep 2026). In the sheet, a 1 against a question means the child answered it incorrectly and 0 means correctly. Where a competency is flagged All correct or All incorrect, that flag is applied to every question and shown as a tag next to the result. A child scoring 80% or more on a competency is Phala, 60–79% Phula, and below 60% Kadha.
